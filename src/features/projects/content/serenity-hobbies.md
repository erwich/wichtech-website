Serenity Hobbies is an independent hobby and gaming shop in Norwich, New York. In 2020, when the pandemic closed its doors, a friend reached out for help bringing the store online.

## Keeping the shop connected

The priority was straightforward: let customers browse, place an order, and pay before coming by for curbside pickup. I built the storefront with WordPress and WooCommerce, integrated Stripe payments, and worked with the owner to get the experience right for the shop.

We shaped it around what he needed to keep serving his customers during an uncertain time. That practical beginning set the tone for the work that followed.

## A longer collaboration

I’ve continued supporting the site since that first launch, making improvements with the owner as the business evolves. It’s become a lasting collaboration, with the website growing alongside the shop.

One of the more unexpected rewards has been hearing about inquiries from overseas. The owner puts real care into sourcing hard-to-find items, and people in France and Germany have discovered his listings and called to ask about shipping. It’s a lovely thing to see a local shop’s reach extend that far.

Serenity is a project I value for its place in someone’s everyday work. Years after the initial build, it’s still useful, still cared for, and still giving us reasons to make it a little better.
